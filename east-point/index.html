How East Point Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Many early failures start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Drain and flush the tank every year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and test the relief valve annually so it doesn't seize. Keep the temperature near 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. If you have a tankless unit, descale it yearly where the water is hard to protect the heat exchanger.

Take a look at the age of your water heater. The serial number on the label usually includes the manufacture date, and that date says a lot about what comes next. Most tank heaters make it to 8 to 12 years, heat pump models 10 to 15, and regularly descaled tankless units around 20. If your heater is beyond that point and is also showing rusty water, rumbling, or dampness under the tank, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install after a leak.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to high water pressure or thermal expansion with nowhere to go, both inexpensive fixes. The same drip ignored for months can wear out the valve and stress the tank until a seam gives way, and a small repair becomes a full replacement and a cleanup. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and cold showers.

Common Water Heater Problems in East Point

Every type of water heater has its own weak points, and we work on all of them around East Point. Gas tanks usually give trouble through pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units lose heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
